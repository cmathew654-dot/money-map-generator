# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Primary users are financial advisors and financial planners. They build and refine client money maps before meetings, then use the existing Present mode during meetings on large televisions, conference-room displays, or screen sharing.

Windows laptops with Chrome or Edge are the primary operating environment. macOS with Safari or Chrome is a secondary certification target.

## Product Purpose

Money Map Generator helps an advisor turn a household's income, needs, accounts, positions, distributions, and explanatory notes into an accurate, presentation-ready visual map.

Success means advisors can prepare maps efficiently, trust that financial data will persist and export correctly, and present the finished map without editing controls or avoidable visual defects distracting from the conversation.

## Brand Personality

Calm, authoritative, human.

## Anti-references

- Bloomberg-terminal density
- Consumer-finance gamification
- Generic SaaS-dashboard styling

## Design Principles

1. Financial accuracy and data integrity take priority over decorative polish.
2. Preparation workflows should be efficient, forgiving, and explicit about saving, recovery, and errors.
3. The map is the product; editing controls should support it without visually competing with it.
4. Present mode should preserve the complete map while removing editing chrome and remain legible on large displays and screen sharing.
5. Complex household structures should remain understandable without flattening or hiding important financial details.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Core workflows must be keyboard-operable, expose meaningful names and status messages to assistive technology, maintain appropriate text and control contrast, remain usable at 200% browser zoom, provide visible focus, and never rely on color alone to communicate meaning.

Primary certification covers Windows Chrome and Edge. Secondary certification covers macOS Safari and Chrome.
