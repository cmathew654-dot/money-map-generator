Close the two naming defects: "after-tax" currently names three unrelated things, and one panel label names a mechanism the map prints under a different phrase.

Separately, someone hunting the Data panel for "As needed" (what the map shows) will not find it, because the panel calls it "Monthly account withdrawal."

Output: An inventory of every site each rename touches, Cyril's ruling on which concept keeps the term, and both renames applied with their test fallout recorded as deliberate realignment.

This plan does not decide IA-05. Cyril's advisor vocabulary decides which of the three keeps the word; the plan's job is to put the three options in front of him with their real cost attached, and then execute his answer.
</objective>
