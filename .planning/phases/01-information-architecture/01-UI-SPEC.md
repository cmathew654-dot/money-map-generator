## Section Taxonomy (IA-01, IA-02, IA-03)

Ground truth is the shipped s51 "Ledger" accordion (`src/form/Form.tsx`), not the stale `canvas-first-editor-design.md` plain-form description — per `STATE.md` and `INGEST-CONFLICTS.md`. Five sections, confirmed both in the section-nav labels (`Form.tsx:1459-1465`) and in `data-form-section` DOM attributes:

| Section | Data-form-section | One-sentence purpose | Belongs here | Does NOT belong here |
|---------|--------------------|----------------------|---------------|------------------------|
| Client | `client` | Who this map is for. | Household/plan identity fields (title, year). | Any dollar figure. |
| Income | `income` | Money coming in, and the after-tax total the map is built from. | Income sources (label, amount, mo/yr period), After-Tax Income total. | Account balances; the monthly need number. |
| Accounts | `accounts` | Where money currently sits. | One accordion card per account (name, value, account type, supporting note), plus each account's nested Positions and Sub-accounts (see below). | Income sources; the monthly need number. |
| Need | `need` | The red number the household must cover each month. | Monthly account withdrawal (as-needed amount), monthly need total. | See Fine print finding below — currently misplaced here. |
| Notes | `notes` | Free-text context that isn't a number. | Freeform textarea notes. | Anything with a dollar value. |

This taxonomy is already correct and unambiguous for 4 of 5 sections. The exceptions are the two concrete overlaps below — these are the actual content of IA-02/IA-03 for this phase, not the section list itself.

### Finding 1 (IA-02) — Fine print is filed under the wrong section

**What's shipped:** `FinePrintSection` (Gross/Net footnote line items) renders physically inside `NeedSection`'s JSX (`Form.tsx:523`) and is included in the `need` section's filter-match blob (`Form.tsx:1475-1481`). It has no separate nav entry — only 5 buttons exist in `data-form-sections`.

**Why it's a real overlap, not just nesting:** Fine print's fields are `Gross` / `Net` — a breakdown of an income figure ("The after-tax amount appears in green," `Form.tsx:1229`). That is income-shaped content, physically filed under the section whose one-sentence purpose is "the monthly number the household must cover." A user reading "Need" and finding gross/net income footnotes there is exactly the "unclear what lives where" complaint.

**Recommendation (default, pending Cyril confirmation):** Move Fine print's home to the Income section — it describes an income figure, so it belongs where income figures live. Concretely: render `FinePrintSection` inside `IncomeSection` instead of `NeedSection`, and fold its content into the `income` filter-match blob instead of `need`'s. Nav stays 5 buttons (Fine print is not a top-level destination, same as today) — only its *parent* changes.

**Alternative not recommended:** Promoting Fine print to a 6th top-level nav item. Rejected by default because its content volume (a handful of Gross/Net lines) doesn't warrant a standalone destination, and a 6th button pushes the nav past the "5 sections, no more" mental model IA-02 is establishing.

**Status: PENDING — needs Cyril's one-word confirmation** (same pattern as the two other open rulings this milestone carries). Plan-phase should treat "move Fine print into Income" as the default unless overridden.

### Finding 2 (IA-03) — Positions and Sub-accounts read as duplicates, but aren't

**What's shipped:** Every account card unconditionally renders two nested groups, `PositionRows` (`Position { label, value }`) and `SubAccountRows` (`SubAccount { label, caption?, value }`) — both use the generic field labels "Label" and "Value," both add via a "+ Add {position/sub-account}" button, and both appear on every account regardless of type. Nothing in the UI communicates why there are two near-identical nested-item groups.

**What they actually are (per `src/model/types.ts`):** Positions are a flat value breakdown *within* the account's own total (e.g. splitting one account by holding/carrier). Sub-accounts are a distinct earmarked pool *carved out of* the account, rendered on the map as its own inset shape (model comment: "nested earmarked pool drawn as an inset cylinder — e.g. RMD short-term funds"). These are structurally siblings but semantically different: one is "what this total is made of," the other is "a separate thing living inside this account."

**Recommendation (default, pending Cyril confirmation):** Keep both nested inside Accounts (that placement is correct — neither belongs as a sibling top-level section). Fix the labeling so the distinction is visible without reading code:
- Positions group subhead gets one line of help text: *"Break this account's total into holdings — e.g. by fund or carrier."*
- Sub-accounts group subhead gets one line of help text: *"Carve out a named pool inside this account — e.g. RMD short-term funds. It gets its own shape on the map."*

This is a copy-only fix (no layout change), so it's in-scope for Phase 1 even though field-level visual craft is Phase 3's job — see Copywriting Contract below for exact strings.

**Explicitly out of scope for Phase 1:** whether Positions/Sub-accounts should be conditionally hidden per account type (e.g. hide Positions on a checking account). That's a visibility/behavior question, not a naming question — defer to Phase 2 (Flow) or Phase 3 if it resurfaces.

**Status: PENDING — needs Cyril's one-word confirmation** on the two help-text strings (or his own alternative wording — he knows the advisor vocabulary better than a generic label would).

### Non-issues confirmed (checked, not overlaps)

- **"Supporting note" appears at both the Account level and the Sub-account level.** Same label, different nesting depth — this is the same concept (a free-text caption) reused consistently, not a competing definition. No change needed.
- **After-Tax Income (Income section) vs. Monthly need (Need section).** Different numbers, different sections, no shared vocabulary collision.
