<objective>
Give every field exactly one section, and make the two nested groups inside an account explain themselves.

Purpose: Fine print's fields are Gross and Net — a breakdown of an income figure — filed under the section whose stated purpose is the monthly number the household must cover. That is the "unclear what lives where" complaint in its most concrete form. Separately, every account card renders two near-identical nested groups ("Label"/"Value" plus an add button, twice) with nothing in the UI saying why there are two.

Output: Fine print relocated to Income (editor and filter index together), two help lines that distinguish Positions from Sub-accounts, a Notes empty state, and a filter zero-results state.

All four changes were ruled by Cyril on 2026-08-08. They are work to execute, not questions to reopen.
</objective>
